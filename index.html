Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the self-hosted marketing automation demo.

How does bidirectional real-time data sync work? ▼
Submissions made through the landing page form update the shared client data store instantly. Any opened Campaign Dashboard tab immediately detects new contacts, updates KPI totals, refreshes segment bars, and triggers welcome workflows in real time.
What happens after a contact is captured? ▼
The lead is assigned to their designated interest segment (e.g. Email Marketing, Lead Gen, Automation, or Analytics) and enrolled into the automated 'Welcome Series' campaign with tracked open and click events.
Can contacts be exported for external CRM handoff? ▼
Yes! Both individual and bulk contact exports to CSV are supported with 1 click. The exported CSV file includes names, business emails, companies, segments, registration dates, and subscription statuses ready for Excel, Google Sheets, or Salesforce.
How does Mautic help ensure GDPR and privacy compliance? ▼
Unlike third-party SaaS vendors where customer records are stored on multi-tenant commercial servers, self-hosted Mautic gives you 100% data sovereignty on your private infrastructure with explicit consent checkboxes and 1-click unsubscribe handling.
